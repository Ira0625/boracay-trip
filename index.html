⟢ THE TABLE

where we're eating.

Hand-picked spots from highly-rated local favorites — beachfront views, authentic flavors, every budget covered.

DAY 1 LUNCH
★ 4.8
Chicken Inasal Bacolod
By Aidalicious · near Station 2
Authentic Bacolod-style inasal — flavorful, affordable, beachfront seating. Way better than chain fastfood. Ask for the MP Meal promo.
₱200–300 per person
DAY 1 DINNER
SEAFOOD
D'Talipapa Market
Manggayad · 10-min walk from Station 2
Pick-your-own fresh seafood — talaba, scallops, lapu-lapu. Bring it to a paluto restaurant nearby. Bargain politely & ask for prices upfront.
₱500–700 per person
DAY 2 BRUNCH
★ 4.5
The Sunny Side Cafe
White Beach Path · Station 2
Famous bacon mango grilled cheese, brioche pancakes with chantilly, mango shake. Goes viral for a reason. Get there early to skip the queue.
₱400–600 per person
DAY 2 DINNER
★ 4.4
Hakuna Matata Boracay
Mandarin Nest · Beachfront Station 2
Beachfront restaurant, viral dancing staff (look for "Kuya"). Try the seafood bucket or festive boodle fight for the group.
₱600–900 per person
DAY 3 LUNCH
★ 4.5
Island Chicken Inasal
D'Mall D'Boracay · Balabag
Quick recharge after ATV — juicy chicken inasal, isol, baticulon. Cash and card accepted. Small space, can get cramped at peak.
₱200–300 per person
DAY 3 DINNER
★ 4.7 · FANCY
Red Coconut Restaurant
Red Coconut Hotel · Beachfront Station 2
Best fire dance show in Boracay (book ahead!). Live band, acoustic singers, scallops & grilled squid recommended. ₱1k consumable for front-row.
₱800–1,200 per person
